See the work.
Understand the care.

The project reports completed roof, foundation and structural work. Earlier condition photographs show why careful preservation mattered.

2018

Recognition of a place at risk

Preservation Pennsylvania included Mount Tabor on its At Risk list. A historical designation, not a description of the building today.

2021

A nationally recognized history

The church and cemetery were listed together in the National Register of Historic Places.

2024

Preservation work honored

The project received a Pennsylvania Historic Preservation Rehabilitation Award.

The people behind the place

The record is kept by people.

Family memories from the Gumby family and the Ward/Smith family’s connection to the cemetery helped bring this history into view.

One place. Many lives.

A church and a burial ground.

The nineteenth-century church was a setting for worship, gathering and shared community life. The cemetery holds remembrance, family connections and the lives of United States Colored Troops veterans.
