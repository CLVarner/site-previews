An investment in the places behind the mission.

Give the work room to continue.

The campaign proposes restoration at Odd Fellows, improved storage and research spaces, and a new visitor arrival. These investments serve different parts of the same responsibility: caring for historical materials and making room for people to encounter them.

Your interests can help begin a thoughtful discussion about the campaign. Explore the scope, ask about the investment, and consider giving or pledge options with the team.

Recognition starts with your preferences.

The campaign's recognition plan includes giving societies and naming opportunities. Talk with us about your interests, the approved terms, and current options as you consider a contribution.

Ask how recognition applies to your gift or pledge, and which naming options the campaign can currently discuss. Share your acknowledgment preferences, including any preference for anonymity, before public recognition is arranged. An inquiry does not reserve an opportunity.
