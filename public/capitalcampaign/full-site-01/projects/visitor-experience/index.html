Preserving the Past. Investing in the Future.

A welcoming first step into local history.

The campaign proposes a new main entrance from High Street and an updated museum store. This arrival would introduce visitors to the Society's museum, archives, and work in Cumberland County.

Proposed campaign improvements
See all campaign projects →

An arrival connected with the Society's work.

The proposal would shift the main visitor entrance to High Street through a new visitor center. An updated museum store would be part of that structure, serving as the entry to the museum and archives.

This change connects with proposed collection and research improvements at the Pitt Street facilities. The public arrival introduces people to that work; proposed learning and gathering spaces at Odd Fellows are another part of the campaign.

From arriving to discovering.

A first encounter should help people understand where they are and what they can explore. The proposed visitor spaces would introduce the Society to people with different interests and different connections to the county, including those visiting for the first time.

Questions about the visitor spaces.

Details to help you understand the proposal.

Where is the proposed entrance?

The campaign case proposes a main visitor entrance from High Street, connecting with the Society's museum and archives at its Pitt Street facilities. Contact the campaign for current project details.

Is the new entrance open now?

This page describes the campaign proposal, not a new entrance in operation. For current visiting information, use the Society's main website.

Does the plan include a museum store?

Yes. An updated museum store is part of the proposed visitor structure described in the campaign case. Final scope and names should be confirmed with the campaign team.

Where can I find current hours and visiting information?

Visit the Society's main website for current practical information. This campaign site focuses on the proposed improvements and ways to support them.
