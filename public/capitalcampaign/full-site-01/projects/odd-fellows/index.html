Preserve its character. Make room for discovery.

The Odd Fellows proposal brings restoration together with practical space for learning and gatherings. It would preserve the hall's distinctive character while preparing the building for education and the Society's work.

A restoration shaped around use.

The proposal gives different parts of the building distinct roles, with education, staff work, and community activity under one roof.

First floor: hands-on learning.

A flexible classroom would support field trips, workshops, and participation with educational materials. The plan also includes woodworking and preservation activity, connecting traditional skills with practical learning.

Second floor: the Society's work.

Offices and conference space would provide a base for staff and the administrative work that supports the Society's museum, archives, and education.

Third floor: Odd Fellows Hall.

The hall would be restored for public lectures and community events. The preservation approach described in the campaign case seeks to retain its existing character rather than erase the features that make the space distinctive.

Access between floors.

An exterior elevator is included in the proposal. Its design and the wider access arrangements are part of the project details to discuss with the campaign team.

Learning takes space.

Room to try, ask, and make.

The Society's educational work includes opportunities to encounter historical objects, dress, and trades through participation. The first-floor proposal would provide a flexible classroom and woodworking space for that work.

A place to gather around an activity matters as much as the materials themselves. This campaign would help prepare rooms for hands-on learning while caring for the building that holds them.

Questions about Odd Fellows.

Details to help you understand the proposal.

What is proposed for the building?

The campaign proposal includes first-floor learning and woodworking/preservation spaces, second-floor offices and conference space, restoration of the third-floor hall, and an exterior elevator. These are planned improvements; current details can be discussed with the campaign team.

What would happen to the hall's murals and character?

The campaign case describes a restoration that retains the hall's distinctive existing character. Contact the campaign if you would like to discuss the proposed treatment of particular features. Specific conservation methods need to be confirmed by the project team.

Would the building include an elevator?

An exterior elevator is part of the proposal. Contact the campaign team to discuss the current access plans and project details.

Can I discuss a gift connected with this project?

Yes. Contact the campaign to discuss your interest in Odd Fellows and the approved giving options. A project conversation does not reserve a naming opportunity or automatically restrict a gift to a particular space.

What would you like to help make possible?

Talk with the campaign team about the Odd Fellows plans, your interests, and options for a larger gift or pledge.
