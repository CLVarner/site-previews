Privacy information.

About this prototype preview.

The forms on this prototype are demonstrations. Information entered into them stays on the current page and is not sent, saved, or used to create a subscription, inquiry, or donation. Use sample details when reviewing the forms.

Giving and campaign emails.

The giving page previews the intended handoff to an approved campaign donation form. No payment is processed here. Newsletter controls demonstrate the signup layout; they do not add an address to a list.

The homepage concept video.

No YouTube player or remote thumbnail loads until you choose to load the video. That choice connects to YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player, which may process connection and browser information under its own practices. Closing the player removes it from this page. The separate YouTube link opens YouTube directly.

The future campaign website.

The live campaign privacy notice will describe the actual services, information handling, and choices that apply to inquiries, donations, campaign emails, and website measurement. That notice requires review before those services go live.
