Before you get in touch.

Questions about your next step.

Do I need to decide on a gift amount first?

No. You can begin with a question about the campaign or the way you would like to take part. The inquiry form does not require a gift amount.

Can I discuss a pledge?

Yes. Choose the pledge topic to ask about approved terms and next steps. Sending the inquiry does not create a pledge agreement.

Can I ask about a particular project or recognition option?

Yes. Tell us about your interests. The team can discuss the campaign's current plans and approved options. An inquiry does not reserve naming rights or automatically restrict a gift.

Can I contact someone directly instead of using the form?

Yes. Use the campaign email address or phone number listed on this page.

Will this inquiry subscribe me to updates?

No. Campaign email signup is a separate choice. If the inquiry form includes an optional updates checkbox, select it only if you want campaign emails.
