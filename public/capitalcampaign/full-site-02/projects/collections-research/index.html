Preserving the Past. Investing in the Future.

Care for the stories still waiting to be found.

Historical materials need care before they can become part of someone's discovery. The campaign would improve storage, research spaces, and the work areas where the Society prepares collections for use.

Care makes discovery possible.

The Society's collection supports museum, research, and educational work. As it grows, the proposal addresses both where historical materials are stored and where people prepare and study them.

Collection care is ongoing work. Improved storage, research areas, and preparation spaces would support that responsibility and the people who use the collection.

Three parts of the work.

Store with care

Proposed climate-controlled basement storage would provide space for the growing collection and support its preservation.

Support research

Reconfigured Pitt Street research spaces would support people studying the Society's materials and asking questions about the historical record.

Prepare for discovery

Processing prepares and organizes materials for use; digitization creates digital versions through work such as scanning. Dedicated areas in Todd Hall would support staff and volunteers doing this work.

Questions about collections and research.

Details to help you understand the proposal.

Would every item become available online?

The proposal includes digitization workspaces; it does not establish that every item will be digitized or made available online. The campaign invests in spaces supporting the Society's ongoing collection work.

How would collection care connect with research?

Storage supports the care of historical materials. Preparation and research spaces support the work of organizing, studying, and encountering them. The proposed improvements address these different functions together.

Where can I find information about researching at CCHS today?

Use the Society's main website for current research information, or contact the Society with a specific question. This campaign page describes proposed improvements.

Can I discuss a gift connected with collection care?

Yes. Contact the campaign to discuss your interests and the approved giving options. Reading this project page does not automatically restrict a gift to collection care.
