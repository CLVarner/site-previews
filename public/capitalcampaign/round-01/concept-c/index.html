The proposed investment

A clear plan for the next chapter.

Each part of the campaign supports the same purpose: a permanent home for local history, with more ways to encounter and learn from it.

01

Restore the Odd Fellows Building & Hall

Bring new purpose to a historic landmark while preserving the hall’s distinctive character.

  • Flexible spaces for classrooms, workshops and preservation learning.
  • Restored hall space for lectures and community events.
  • A proposed exterior elevator to improve access between floors.
02

Protect collections. Improve discovery.

Give the county’s stories the space and care they need—and help researchers, staff and volunteers share them.

  • Expanded climate-controlled collections storage.
  • Improved research spaces in the Pitt Street building.
  • Scanning, digitization and processing spaces in Todd Hall.
03

Welcome visitors. Inspire learners.

Create a more welcoming visitor experience and make room for hands-on connections to our shared past.

  • A new visitor center and reimagined arrival experience.
  • A museum store and new gallery space.
  • Educational spaces connecting historic collections with active learning.

A few things to know

Ask us more.

What will this campaign make possible?

The proposed project restores the Odd Fellows Building, expands collections storage, reimagines the visitor arrival experience, and adds spaces for education, research and digitization.

Why does this matter for Cumberland County?

The campaign connects preservation with participation: a home for the county’s stories, a place to encounter them, and spaces where students and families can learn through hands-on experiences.

Can I discuss a pledge or a different kind of gift?

Yes. The supplied campaign plan includes pledges, cash or check, gifts of stock or appreciated securities, and corporate matching gifts. The campaign team will confirm the instructions and terms for each option.

Are naming and recognition opportunities available?

The campaign plan includes donor recognition and naming opportunities. Speak with the campaign team to confirm current availability, gift terms and recognition preferences.

What are the campaign goal and project timeline?

Campaign figures and construction milestones will be shared here once confirmed. This design preview does not present draft progress figures as current results.
