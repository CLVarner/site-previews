The organization behind the campaign

Cumberland County Historical Society.

A place to encounter Cumberland County’s history through museum, archive, research, and educational work.

History and community meet here.

The campaign focuses on proposed improvements to the places supporting the Society’s work: Odd Fellows restoration, collection and research spaces, and a new visitor arrival.

Current research and visiting information will link to the Society’s main website in the finished campaign site. This local preview keeps those connections within the prototype.
