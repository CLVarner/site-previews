A place to make connections.

History becomes personal when we encounter it.

A class in the Reading Room. A gathering in Odd Fellows Hall. A chance to try on historical dress at a Block Party. These are some of the ways people encounter Cumberland County's history through the Society.

As the collection and the community's stories grow, the Society is planning spaces for collection care, research, and hands-on learning. Whether your county connections are new or lifelong, there is something here to discover.

What your support helps advance.

Restore a place. Care for collections. Welcome discovery.

Three connected investments would improve the spaces behind the Society's preservation, research, and educational work.

01 / Restore

Restore Odd Fellows

Preserve the hall's distinctive character while preparing the building for hands-on learning, staff work, and community gatherings. The proposal includes a classroom, woodworking and preservation spaces, and an exterior elevator.

Explore the Odd Fellows plan →
02 / Protect

Protect collections and research

Add climate-controlled storage and improve the spaces where staff and volunteers scan, process, and prepare historical materials for discovery.

Explore collection care →
03 / Welcome

Welcome visitors

Create a proposed High Street entrance and updated museum store, connecting arrivals with the Society's museum and archives.
