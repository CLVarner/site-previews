Questions about giving.

Questions about your next step.

How is this campaign different from membership or annual support?

This capital campaign supports plans for buildings and spaces: Odd Fellows restoration, collection and research improvements, and a new visitor arrival. If you want to confirm how your contribution will be designated, contact the campaign before giving.

Is a pledge the same as a recurring donation?

No. A pledge is a commitment under agreed terms; a recurring donation is a payment arrangement. Contact the campaign to discuss a pledge and review the actual options available through the donation form.

Can I talk with someone before deciding how to give?

Yes. Send a campaign inquiry about your questions or preferred method. You do not need to decide on an amount to begin a conversation.

Can I ask about recognition or naming?

Yes. The leadership-giving page introduces that conversation. Naming and recognition depend on approved terms and current availability; an inquiry does not reserve an opportunity.

Have a question about your gift?

Ask about the campaign, a giving method, or a pledge. Tell us what you would like to discuss so your question can reach the right person.
