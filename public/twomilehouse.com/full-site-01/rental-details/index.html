Good to know

A few questions, answered.

Start here, then bring the details of your plans to a venue inquiry.

Do we rent the entire property?＋

Yes. Your rental covers the entire Two Mile House property, rather than individual rooms. We can explain the property’s guidelines as you plan your event.

What furniture is included?＋

The rental includes 165 folding chairs, five round tables, four 4-foot tables, ten 6-foot tables, seven high-top/bistro tables, six card tables and easels.

Does the venue provide catering or event planning?＋

No. Two Mile House is a venue-only rental. You arrange catering and other event services separately. We can answer questions about the property and its guidelines.

Can we hold an event other than a wedding?＋

Yes. The property is also used for showers, birthday parties and holiday gatherings. Contact us to discuss the event you have in mind.

How do we obtain rental pricing?＋

Contact us for current pricing and rental terms.
